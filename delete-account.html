Delete your split account

შენი split ანგარიშის წაშლა

From inside the app

Open Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account is removed immediately.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email splitapp.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, avatar, and your IBAN.
  • Your authentication record — you can no longer sign in.
  • Receipt images you uploaded.
  • Any payment-request links you created or that were addressed to you, including the copy of your name and IBAN stored in them.

What is kept, and why

Expenses and settlements you shared with other people are not deleted. They are part of other members' financial records, and removing them would silently change what those people owe each other. Your entries stay in those groups under a placeholder name, no longer linked to you or to any personal data.

If you need something beyond this, contact us at splitapp.support@gmail.com.
