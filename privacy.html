Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-13

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data split-app ("split-app", "we", "us", "our") collects when you use our mobile application and related services (together, the "App"), how we use and protect that data, and the rights you have over it.

split-app is a bill- and expense-splitting application for groups (trips, households, events). It helps you record shared expenses, see who owes whom, and prepare bank-transfer details so you can settle up using your own banking app. split-app is not a bank, payment service, or money transmitter, and money never passes through the App. See our Terms & Conditions for more on how settlements work.

We are based in Georgia (the country), and the App is offered primarily to users in Georgia. Although Georgian law governs this policy, we apply privacy practices modelled on the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as a matter of good practice, including the user rights described in Section 8.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for the App is the split-app team.

  • Privacy / general contact: splitapp.support@gmail.com

If you have any question about this policy or about how your data is handled, you can reach us at either address above.

2. Data we collect

We only collect data that the App actually needs to work. The categories below match the data our systems store.

2.1 Account and profile data

  • First name and surname (required) — both are needed to use the App, and together they form the display name shown to other members of your groups. The surname is required because settling up ends with someone entering your name in their own banking app and checking it against the account holder before sending money; a first name alone is not enough to verify a recipient.
  • Email address — used to create and secure your account (email sign-in or Google sign-in).
  • Phone number (optional) — only if you choose to provide it.
  • Avatar image (optional) — if you set a profile picture, including one provided by Google sign-in.
  • Bank account numbers (IBANs) (optional) — one or more Georgian IBANs you may add so that other members of your groups can send you money from their own bank app. You may add several accounts and mark one as your default; members paying you choose which of them to send to, so that the transfer can stay within their own bank. You may add an optional short label to an account (for example, "salary") to tell two accounts apart. Providing an IBAN is entirely voluntary; you can use the App without one, and you can remove one at any time. From each IBAN we automatically derive only your bank code (for example, TBC, Bank of Georgia, Credo or Terabank) to show the bank's name and open the correct bank app at settlement time.
  • Language preference (locale) — Georgian or English.
  • Terms acceptance record — the date and version of the Terms & Conditions you accepted.

2.2 Group and expense data

When you use the App you create and store records such as:

  • Groups you create or join (name, emoji/icon, currency, invite code, settings).
  • Group members, including "ghost" members you add by name for people who have not yet installed the App.
  • Expenses — description, amount, currency, who paid, how the cost is split, category, date, and any notes.
  • Splits — each member's owed share of an expense.
  • Settlements — records of who paid whom, the amount, the method (TBC app, Bank of Georgia app, cash, or other), and confirmation status.
  • Activity log — a feed of group events (expense added, member joined, settlement recorded, and similar).

2.3 Receipt images and parsed data

  • Receipt photos — if you scan or upload a receipt, the image is stored so it can be attached to the related expense.
  • Parsed receipt data — the structured text extracted from a receipt (merchant, date, line items, amounts, total). See Section 4 for how receipts are processed.

2.4 Technical data

To operate, secure, and synchronise the App, our backend provider processes standard technical information such as authentication tokens and basic request metadata. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or device identifiers for tracking, and we do not run advertising trackers in the App.

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to provide and improve the App:

  • to create and secure your account and sign you in;
  • to let you create groups, record shared expenses, and calculate balances;
  • to show your name, avatar, and — to members of your shared groups — your IBAN(s), so the group can settle up;
  • to prepare transfer details (recipient IBAN and amount) and open your banking app when you choose to settle;
  • to extract text from receipts you scan (Section 4);
  • to sync your data across your devices and the devices of your group members;
  • to maintain security, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Receipt scanning and third-party AI processing

When you use the receipt-scanning feature, the photo you provide is sent through our secure backend to a third-party artificial-intelligence service — Google Gemini (Google) — solely to extract the text and figures printed on the receipt (merchant, date, line items, totals) and return them as structured data.

  • The image is sent only for this text-extraction purpose. It is not used to build an advertising profile of you.
  • We configure the service so that the receipt image is processed only to produce the parsed result and is not retained by the AI provider beyond what is needed to perform the parsing.
  • The parsed text is returned to the App and stored with your expense so you can review and edit it.
  • The receipt image you scan is stored in our own backend storage and attached to your expense until you or we delete it (see Section 6 and Section 7).

Google processes this data as our service provider. Because the AI service is operated by a global provider, this processing may involve transfer of the image outside Georgia. We rely on the provider's contractual and technical safeguards for such processing.

5. Who can see your data

  • You can always see and manage your own profile and the groups you belong to.
  • Members of a group you share can see your display name and avatar, the shared expenses, splits, settlements, and activity in that group, and — if you provided any — your IBAN(s), because they need them to send you money.
  • Your IBANs are visible only to people who share at least one group with you. This restriction is enforced at the database level by Row-Level Security (RLS); strangers and users who do not share a group with you cannot read your IBANs, and nobody but you can add, change or remove one.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure or process data on our behalf (see Section 9) access data only to provide their service to us.

We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your personal data.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend provider (Postgres database, authentication, file storage, and realtime sync), hosted in a European Union data region. Receipt images are stored in Supabase storage. Access to your records is restricted by Row-Level Security policies so that you and your fellow group members see only what you are entitled to see.

7. Data retention

  • We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account exists.
  • We keep group, expense, settlement, and receipt data for as long as the relevant group exists or as needed to provide the App to you and your group members.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data associated with it, except where we must retain certain records to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve disputes. Note that expenses and settlements you created may remain visible to other group members as part of that group's shared history, in a form that no longer identifies you.
  • Receipt images sent to the AI service for parsing are not retained by the AI provider beyond parsing (Section 4); the copy stored in our backend is deleted when you delete the expense or your account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (you can edit most profile data directly in the App);
  • Delete your account and associated personal data;
  • Export / port a copy of your data in a common format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave (for example, by removing an IBAN or declining to use receipt scanning).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at splitapp.support@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

9. Third-party service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run the App:

  • Supabase — database hosting, authentication, file storage, and realtime sync.
  • Google — Google sign-in (authentication) and Google Gemini (receipt text-extraction).

These providers process data on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not permit them to use your data for their own advertising purposes.

When you choose to settle a debt, the App opens your own banking app (such as TBC or Bank of Georgia). Those banks are independent and have their own privacy policies; any transfer you make happens entirely within your bank's app and is governed by your bank's terms, not ours.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at splitapp.support@gmail.com and we will delete it. Some jurisdictions set a higher age of digital consent; where a higher age applies, that age governs.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, authenticated access, and Row-Level Security that limits each user to their own data and their groups' shared data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to address any vulnerabilities promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
