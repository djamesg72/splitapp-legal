1. What split-app is — and what it is not

split-app is a tool for recording and splitting shared expenses among members of a group and for keeping track of who owes whom. To help you settle those balances, the App can prepare transfer details (the recipient's IBAN and an amount) and open your own banking app so you can make a payment yourself.

split-app is not a bank, payment institution, payment service provider, money transmitter, or escrow service. We do not hold, transfer, receive, or touch your money at any point. Any payment you make happens entirely within your own bank's application and is subject to your bank's terms and controls — not ours.

2. The settlement feature — read this carefully

This is the most important section of these Terms.

2.1 How settlement works

When you choose to settle a balance:

  1. The App shows you the amount and the recipient, together with the recipient's IBAN (which that recipient voluntarily added to their own profile).
  2. The App may copy the IBAN and amount to your clipboard and open the appropriate banking app (for example, TBC or Bank of Georgia), detected from the recipient's IBAN.
  3. You complete (or decline) the transfer inside your own bank app.
  4. Back in split-app, you can record that you sent the payment; the recipient then confirms they received it.

2.2 You must verify the recipient before sending

Before you send any transfer, you must check the recipient's first name and last name as displayed in your banking app and confirm they match the person you intend to pay. A bank executes a transfer based on the account number; the account holder's name shown by your bank is your safeguard against sending money to the wrong person.

2.3 split-app prepares details only — and is not liable for misdirected funds

split-app prepares transfer details as a convenience only. We are not a party to any payment you make, we do not validate that an IBAN belongs to the intended person, and we do not verify, guarantee, or process any transfer.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, split-app is not responsible or liable for funds sent to a wrong or unintended account, including where:

  • an IBAN was entered incorrectly by its own owner when they added it to their profile;
  • you sent money before verifying the recipient's name in your bank app;
  • a transfer failed, was delayed, was reversed, or was charged a fee by a bank; or
  • a recipient did not receive, or disputes receiving, a payment.

Responsibility for any actual payment lies between you, the recipient, and the banks involved.

2.4 In-app balances are bookkeeping records, not proof of payment

Balances, "settlements", and "confirmations" shown in the App are bookkeeping records that users create about each other. Marking a settlement as sent or confirmed is a record kept for the group's convenience. It is not evidence that a bank transfer was actually executed, received, or cleared. Only your bank's records and statements prove an actual transfer. Disputes about whether money was truly sent or received must be resolved between the users involved and their banks.
